# GROUPE TAKATAK brand reference

Use this for anything visual or written for TAKATAK: websites, dashboards, apps, emails, social posts, images, documents. Source: the owner's official logo and banner (Oct 2026).

## Identity

- **Name:** GROUPE TAKATAK (short form TAKATAK). Website: takatak.ca. Based in Montréal, Canada.
- **Tagline:** Digital Solutions. Real Results.
- **Headlines in use:** "Your Business Stronger Online", "Grow · Automate · Scale", "Modern Websites for a Bigger Tomorrow".
- **Pillars:** Websites · Apps · Marketing · Automation · AI · Growth.
- **Services (in this order):** Websites (custom & SEO) · Hosting (fast & secure) · Domains (your brand online) · Marketing (strategy & ads) · Social media (content & growth) · Local listings (get found) · CRM & leads (turn visitors into customers) · AI tools (work smarter) · Marketplace (sell & scale) · Support (always here).

## Logo

- **Official mark:** a white "T" with an electric-blue upward arrow sweeping around it, inside a glowing blue circle on deep navy, over a faint dotted world map. Wordmark: "GROUPE" small and wide-spaced, "TAKATAK" large in bold silver-white caps with blue accents in the A's, then "DIGITAL SOLUTIONS" and "REAL RESULTS" in cyan.
- **Use it on dark backgrounds.** Keep clear space around it. Don't recolour, stretch or add effects.
- **Retired:** the silver-and-gold "TK" mark (`takatak-v1/public/img/imgtak/*.png`). Replace it wherever it appears.
- **Files:** the owner uploads the official files to `knowledgeAI/brand/` (`logo-circle.png`, `logo-wordmark.png`, `banner.png`). Use those, never a re-drawn copy.

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy (ground) | `#060D1F` | Dark backgrounds, hero sections |
| Midnight | `#0B1B3D` | Cards and panels on navy |
| Electric blue | `#1F8BFF` | Primary accent, glows, highlights on dark |
| Brand blue (on light) | `#1565D8` | Buttons and links on white (white text passes contrast) |
| Cyan | `#29C3FF` | "Real Results", small highlights, charts |
| White | `#F5F8FC` | Text on dark, light backgrounds |
| Silver | `#B8C4D3` | Secondary text on dark, borders |

Green, red and amber are for status only (success, error, warning), never as the brand colour.

## Type and style

- Headings: bold geometric sans-serif in caps or title case (Montserrat ExtraBold is the closest free match). Small labels in wide-spaced caps, like "GROUPE" and "DIGITAL SOLUTIONS".
- Body: a clean sans-serif (Montserrat or Inter at regular weight).
- Look: dark navy with blue light, clean dashboards, a Montréal skyline at night, device mockups. Confident, modern, professional.
- Voice: short, direct, benefit first. Bilingual: English and Canadian French.
