## Measurements to obtain

| Measurement | How to obtain it | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perimeter P, mm | Wrap a paper strip around all six sides at the intended label height, mark the overlap, flatten and measure | Full wrap width; start with P minus a 2 mm gap and paper-fit it |
| Flat-face width F, mm | Measure only the straight, completely flat part of one face; exclude both rounded corners | Complete barcode fit, including white space |
| Usable label height H, mm | Measure the straight-sided area between the shoulder and base curves | Label height and barcode length |
| Actual honey net weight, g | Weigh a filled jar and subtract the empty jar/lid tare | The retail quantity; never calculated automatically from 288 mL |

Do not multiply F by six to guess P: rounded corners add distance. Capacity alone cannot establish the jar dimensions. Confirm whether the advertised volume is brimful or recommended fill capacity.

## Label workflow

1. Make a paper strip at the measured height and P minus a 2 mm gap. Wrap it onto the actual jar without adhesive; check it does not touch either curved edge.
2. In the label studio choose **Hexagonal jar**. Enter width, height and **Flat-face width mm**. The shape and face measurement save with the product layout; older layouts remain readable.
3. Choose a barcode orientation and printer resolution. Ladder orientation can help a narrow face by turning the symbol. The studio checks the complete symbol, white space and readable digits, with an extra 2 mm application allowance on each side. This allowance is an application fit check, not a barcode-quality certification or a legal minimum edge distance.
4. If the symbol cannot fit, the studio blocks the label instead of shrinking the bars or letting them cross a corner. Consider a different orientation/resolution, a shorter internal SKU, or a jar with a wider flat panel. The assigned retail product number itself must not be shortened.
5. Centre the complete barcode on one flat face when applying. Keep it away from the wrap seam. This fit check does not map every fold or position every text panel; inspect the whole paper wrap and keep required text readable.
6. Print at **Actual size / 100%**, apply one adhesive sample, and scan the finished jar from several normal checkout angles. Obtain any barcode verification the retailer requests.
