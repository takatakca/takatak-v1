# Market and marketing claims

Read on 2026-10-23. Research only. **Not legal advice.** **FACT** = what a dated source says. **INFERENCE** = our
reading. Most market sources below are vendors' own pages or roundups (marked **secondary**): useful for orientation,
not proof.

## Bottom line (INFERENCE)

- The market already has AI marketing and listings tools for restaurants, some tied to a POS. "The first AI backend" is
  very likely **not true** in its literal meaning, and cannot be proven. Do not use it in the Clover listing, ads or
  sales emails.
- What TAKATAK can truthfully claim is narrower and stronger for Québec: **installed and billed through Clover, menu and
  hours taken from Clover, French first, the merchant approves every post, direct connections to Google and Meta, and an
  "Order" button that points to the restaurant's own Clover online ordering.**

## Why the "first AI backend" slogan is a problem

- **FACT.** Competition Act s.74.01(1)(a): a representation to the public that is false or misleading in a material
  respect is reviewable conduct; the "general impression … as well as its literal meaning" counts. s.74.01(1)(b):
  performance claims need "an adequate and proper test", proof on the person making it.
  Source: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-34/ (current to 2026-09-30).
- **FACT.** Clover: listing descriptions must not mislead (Developer Program Policies); listing information must be "true
  and accurate" (Developer Terms s.13.1).
- **FACT (secondary).** A Canadian Marketing Association playbook (July 2025) reports the Competition Bureau's view that
  AI claims are treated like any other claim and must be clear; a 2026 law-firm note reports the Bureau saying there is
  no AI exception to the deceptive-marketing rules.
  Sources: https://thecma.ca/docs/default-source/default-document-library/CMA-AI-Playbook_AI-Washing.pdf ;
  https://www.blg.com/fr/insights/2026/10/ai-transparency-the-competition-bureau-weighs-in (read 2026-10-23).
- **INFERENCE.** "First", "only", "#1", "the most advanced", "guaranteed more orders", "Google partner", "Meta partner",
  "endorsed by Clover" are all claims we cannot back today. "AI" itself is fine when the feature really uses AI and the
  claim says what it does.

## Words and ideas to avoid (owner rules + platform rules)

- "premier", "seul", "n° 1", "first", "only", "#1", "best", "garanti", "guaranteed" (unproven superlatives).
- Any suggestion of partnership or endorsement by Clover, Google, Meta, Pinterest; their names or logos in our app name
  or icon.
- "virtuel", "cuisine fantôme", "ghost kitchen", "virtual brand": TAKATAK brands are real restaurants.
- "24h", "24/7", "ouvert 24 heures" (owner rule; Google allows 24-hour hours only when true).
- Inside a post for a restaurant: invented prices, discounts, reviews, awards, allergen or health claims.
- Mentioning one delivery platform in another platform's context (owner rule); the TAKATAK app does not talk about
  delivery platforms at all.

Only list the channels that work at submission time. Limits: tagline ≤255 characters, 3–5 benefits ≤100 characters each.

**Tagline (FR, ~190 characters).** Publiez votre menu Clover, vos heures et vos publications sur Google, Facebook et
Instagram, en français d'abord, depuis un seul tableau de bord. Vous approuvez tout avant la mise en ligne.

**Tagline (EN, ~160 characters).** Publish your Clover menu, hours and posts to Google, Facebook and Instagram from one
dashboard, French first. You approve everything before it goes live.

**Benefits (FR).**
1. Votre menu Clover à jour sur Google, sans retaper un seul prix
2. Heures mises à jour automatiquement depuis Clover sur Google (jusqu’à 48 h de délai)
3. Publications Facebook et Instagram rédigées avec l'IA, approuvées par vous
4. Un bouton « Commander » vers votre commande en ligne Clover
5. En français d'abord, en anglais aussi

**Benefits (EN).**
1. Your Clover menu kept up to date on Google, no retyping prices
2. Hours sent from Clover to Google automatically (changes can take up to 48 h to appear)
3. Facebook and Instagram posts drafted with AI, approved by you
4. An "Order" button that leads to your Clover online ordering
5. French first, English too

Benefit 2 names only channels where hours sync really works (Google today; Facebook hours writes are UNVERIFIED;
Instagram has no hours API). Google can take 24–48 h to show a change, pushes are batched, and sync stops when Clover
billing is lapsed or inactive (ARCHITECTURE §5.1, §7), so never write "always current" / « toujours à jour ».
Benefit 3 stays only if the ROADMAP P1.17 AI guardrails pass at submission; otherwise replace it.

**Setup requirements to state in the description.** A Google Business Profile you own or manage; a Facebook Page and an
Instagram professional account linked to it (for those channels); menu items in Clover Inventory; photos uploaded in
TAKATAK (Clover's API does not appear to share item photos; to confirm with Clover).

## Pricing reference (INFERENCE)

- Owner pattern for Clover apps: 14-day trial, then CAD $50/month (memory of 2026-10-17 decisions; see
  [OWNER_DECISIONS.md](OWNER_DECISIONS.md) D5).
- Reported market prices for listings sync are higher per location (Marqii US$74.99–149.99 per location per month,
  secondary source). CAD $50/month for one location with Google + Facebook + Instagram is competitive.
- Clover keeps 30% of app revenue: CAD $50 → about CAD $35 to TAKATAK before taxes and costs (AI tokens, hosting).
