# Raw material for takatak.ca videos and pages

The owner asked every product agent to send the raw material for the new takatak.ca. We turn it into
short videos (5–8 seconds, so we spend few credits) and reuse them on the site, social media, ads and
Google profiles.

## What to send, per product

| Product (repo) | Raw material wanted |
| --- | --- |
| Food Hub (`foodhubca`) | 2–3 screen recordings: a new order arriving and being accepted, the kitchen screen, the menu sync. Use the demo/test store only. |
| Clover suite (`C4ISSE`, `T4CTILE`, `4CCOUNTING`, `TR4CKER`, `ST4FF`, `M4RKETING`, `LOY4LTY`, `REW4RD`, `CH4T`) | One screen recording or 3 screenshots per app on a Clover sandbox merchant: the main screen and the one action that sells it. |
| QMAPS (`qmaps`) | A recording of a search ending on a business profile; the source and licence of every photo on qmaps.ca. |
| MIMT (`mimt`, `mimtcaapp`) | The web app call/text screens with demo numbers only; any approved launch visuals. |
| Academy (`ACADEMYTAKATAK`) | A lesson page and a course list; the public address once it exists. |
| SEO TAKATAK (`SEOTAKATAK`) | The action-plan screen with a demo site. |
| TAK bot / AI (`takatak-v1`, `knowledgeAI`) | Approved logo files (`knowledgeAI/brand/`) and any brand video. |
| Facturations (`Facturations`) | Nothing until it is cleared for public use. |

## Rules (public repositories)

- No merchant, customer or employee data on screen: no real store names, orders, amounts, phone numbers
  or emails. Use sandbox/demo accounts. The read-only merchant `RNVTBYMCKXEJ1` is never shown.
- Each delivery platform appears only on its own (a DoorDash clip never shows Uber, and the reverse).
- Real people only with a licence or written consent; say where each photo or video comes from.
- Put files in `takatak-v1/public/media/raw/<product>/` on a branch, or in the Google Drive folder
  « 00 — Opérations TAKATAK », and leave a line on the agent board.

How to make a recording: 1280×720 or larger, 10–30 seconds, no cursor jumps, French UI first, then English.
